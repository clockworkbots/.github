Clockwork Bots is an AI and software engineering agency. We build AI agents, agentic workflows, custom software and full-stack apps for businesses.

### What we build

- AI agents for the work your team repeats
- Business process automation that takes the manual steps out of your week
- Backends and APIs for products that need to scale
- AI added to the systems you already run
- Custom software and full-stack apps

### Our work

Systems for Abbas Tech and DJI Store.Pk, and our own app WatermarkMyID, each with a case study at [clockworkbots.com/work](https://clockworkbots.com/work).
